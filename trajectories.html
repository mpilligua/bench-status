How a trajectory is made

  1. Initialisation (no training): per frame, DA3 3D points inside the vehicle mask; frames registered to each other with RANSAC on DVLT descriptors; gaps interpolated, frames outside the registered range extrapolated at constant velocity.
  2. New initialisation: extrapolation bug fixed; per-frame lateral correction to fit the mask; planar motion (rotation only about the vertical, centre height on one smooth road slope); vehicle points straight from the dense DA3 depth inside the mask at any distance (the per-frame DA3 clouds stop at 50 m) with erosion proportional to the mask size; RANSAC thresholds proportional to distance; a remaining jump splits the registration instead of discarding it; vehicles that still fall back to per-frame depth are smoothed over time; a vehicle that drives straight (or is static) gets one constant heading.
  3. After training: the pose of every training frame is optimised together with the vehicle's 3D model, photometrically. Trained from the initialisation before the fix.
