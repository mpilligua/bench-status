Vehicle point clouds (initialisation)

← Benchmark status · Vehicle gallery · Init vs GT videos · Trajectories · 3D points of every vehicle from the new initialisation, before training

The cloud is what the initialisation registers for each vehicle: DA3 points inside its mask from every frame, put together with the registered poses (or, for the depth fallback, centred per frame). Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The vehicle's heading is +x (red axis), up is +z (blue); grid squares are 1 m. Up to 4000 points shown.
