Shadow masks (background training)

The background is trained with the vehicle mask, a 10 px ring around it and the shadow of every vehicle we model. Shadows of anything else (parked cars, poles, hedges) stay in the background, since they are static and the background can learn them.

  1. Detect dark road next to each modelled vehicle: darker than the same ground point in other frames (temporal), or darker than the bright road nearby in the same frame, checked against the road plane.
  2. Not on other vehicles: drop shadow pixels on vehicles we do not model (Grounded-SAM2 "car. truck. bus." detections that do not overlap our masks), then keep only shadow pieces still connected to their own vehicle.
  3. Must move with its vehicle: put each shadow pixel on the road plane and look at that ground point in the other frames where its vehicle is not around. If it is still dark in most of them, the shadow belongs to something static and is dropped.
